## Key Dates to Watch

- **Park entry** — ✅ confirmed: neither Yellowstone nor Grand Teton requires a timed-entry reservation in 2026; just pay ~$35/vehicle (or use an $80 America the Beautiful pass) at the gate. **Each $35 is good for 7 days**, so Saturday's Grand Teton fee also covers Sunday's wildlife drive — keep the receipt. ⚠️ **New in 2026: a $100-per-person surcharge for non-U.S.-residents** — almost certainly moot for this crew, but worth one question in the chat rather than a surprise at the booth
- 🚧 **Moose-Wilson Road closed Sept 8 – Nov 15, 2026** — the back way from Teton Village into the park. Every park drive goes through Jackson instead
- 🔥 **Fire season is live — and it's the September risk this plan never named.** Grand Teton's Stage 1 fire restrictions lifted **Aug 31** with fire danger still high, and the **Fish Creek fire** closed **US-26/287 east of Moran toward Dubois** in early September with no reopening date published. **That is not our route** — every park drive goes north on US-89/191/287 to the South Entrance — but it is the first thing that would reroute us, and smoke is what would flatten Sunday's sunrise at Schwabacher. Fold it into the same day-of check as Craig Pass *(re-checked 9/8)*
- **Elk rut** — peaks mid-September through early October
- **Fall foliage** — aspens turn gold late September in the Tetons
- **Grand Teton road closures** — most seasonal closures land mid-October and we're well ahead of them; **the exception is Moose-Wilson, already closed** (above)
- **Bridger Gondola** — last day of the season is **Sept 13**; we land the 17th. The **Aerial Tram** runs to **Oct 4**, so Sunday's Corbet's waffles are safe
