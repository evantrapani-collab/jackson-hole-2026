- **Park entry** — ✅ confirmed: neither Yellowstone nor Grand Teton requires a timed-entry reservation in 2026; just pay ~$35/vehicle (or use an $80 America the Beautiful pass) at the gate. **Each $35 is good for 7 days**, so Saturday's Grand Teton fee also covers Sunday's wildlife drive — keep the receipt. ⚠️ **New in 2026: a $100-per-person surcharge for non-U.S.-residents** — almost certainly moot for this crew, but worth one question in the chat rather than a surprise at the booth
