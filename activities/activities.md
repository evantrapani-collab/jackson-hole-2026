# Activities

The schedule is full — Friday is booked dawn to dusk, Saturday is a Teton hike plus the LSU game, Sunday is Yellowstone plus the Saints (missed live). This is what's on the plan, and what's here for a future trip.

## On the plan

- **Grand Teton sunrise safari** — Friday 8 AM, Teton Expeditions, 4 hrs ✅ booked
- **Snake River whitewater** — Friday 1 PM, JHWW, Class 2–3 ✅ booked
- **Teton hike** — Saturday: Jenny Lake shuttle boat to the West Dock, then Hidden Falls → Inspiration Point → Cascade Canyon to the forks and back, **~9 mi round trip**. Boots
- **Yellowstone** — Sunday: Grand Prismatic overlook (a **1.6-mile round-trip walk** from the Fairy Falls trailhead — boots), Old Faithful + the Upper Geyser Basin loop, West Thumb on the way out
- **Aerial Tram + Corbet's Cabin** — Sunday afternoon, walkable from the house. Runs to Oct 4, 8:30–5, ~$55 at the window
- **Downtown Jackson** — antler arches, western shops, Spirits & Spice, a Pearl Street sloshie

## If a day gets scrubbed

- **National Museum of Wildlife Art** — world-class, over the elk refuge. The indoor move
- **Grand Teton instead of Yellowstone** if Craig Pass closes — Signal Mountain, String Lake. An hour closer and needs no new plan

## Doesn't fit

- 🚫 **Bridger Gondola** — season ends Sep 13, four days before we land. The tram is the lift
- 🚫 **Moose-Wilson Road** — closed for construction through Nov 15
- 🚫 **Wildlife drive** (Schwabacher, Oxbow Bend, Mormon Row) — cut when Sunday became the Yellowstone day
- 🚫 **Northern Yellowstone** — Norris, Mammoth, Lamar Valley, the canyon waterfalls. All 1.5–3 hrs past our 2:00 PM turnaround. Next trip
- 🚫 **Jackson Hole Rodeo** (season ended Sep 7) · **Saturday farmers market** · **Snow King concerts** (summer only)
- **Fishing, horseback, paragliding** — all excellent here, none of it fits a schedule this full
