🧯 If the DFW connection blows up
Misconnect Playbook ▶
Most of the crew is on one ticket, so AA rebooks free and automatically. The race is 13 seats into a small mountain airport, and the thing to protect is Friday's 8 AM safari (past its refund window), not Thursday night.
  • Tier 0 — prevention: everyone loads the trip in the AA app with push alerts; carry-on if you can; sit toward the front of AA 468 — 43 min is a Skylink sprint
  • ⚠️ Know who's exposed: a few guys booked separately and get no group rebooking protection — names still TBD, and it's on the To-Do list
  • Tier 1 — missed it: the same-day evening DFW→JAC nonstop (~5:50 PM) is the last chance — AA flies JAC twice daily. Everyone rebooks in the app at once while one guy calls AA and one works the gate desk. Hardie + Schick get seats first — they hold the rental cars
  • Tier 2 — no JAC seats: fly DFW→SLC and drive (~5 hrs); one-way SUV rentals SLC→Jackson are routine. Even a 3 PM SLC landing makes dinner at the house
  • Tier 3 — refuse this one: overnighting at DFW. Rule of thumb: before ~6 PM with no confirmed JAC seat, take the SLC routing. Drive before you sleep.
  • Side effect: beds are picked on arrival, and Ciolino has the floor spot by design — don't let a late-landing guy get bumped onto the couch on top of him
