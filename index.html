🌯 Breakfast Burritos — Early-Start Stash

Saturday's 7 AM Yellowstone roll-out and Sunday's 6:30 AM sunrise drive leave no time to cook. Keep a batch of breakfast burritos in the fridge to grab-heat-and-go in the dark.

  • Where — Cowboy Coffee, Pearl Street Bagels, Picnic, D.O.G. (friend's burrito pick), or Hand Fire; order a bulk batch (~1.5/guy ≈ 18–20)
  • When — pick up Friday after rafting so they're stashed before Saturday's alarm (or buy Thursday & freeze)
  • Heads up — restaurant delivery into Teton Village is thin; call ahead & pick up rather than count on same-day delivery
  • Reheat — foil in the oven or microwave; keep salsa/hot sauce at the house; freeze a few as Sunday backup

🛟 DFW Misconnect Playbook

One ticket = AA rebooks everyone free and automatically. The problem is 13 seats, not money — and the thing to protect is Friday's 8 AM van tour (refund window closed), not Thursday.

  • Tier 0 — prevent: all 13 in the AA app w/ push alerts; carry-on only if you can (checked bags chain you to AA's rebooking); sit up front on AA 468 — the 43 min is a Skylink sprint
  • Tier 1 — evening nonstop: second DFW→JAC usually ~5:50 PM (lands ~7:45). All 13 rebook in the app at once while one calls AA + one hits the gate desk. Rental drivers (Hardie + Schick) get seats first — advance party grabs the SUVs, the house, and triggers grocery delivery
  • Tier 2 — no JAC seats: DFW→SLC (many dailies) + one-way rental + ~5-hr drive. Whole group → two one-way SUVs at SLC, cancel the JAC rentals; split group → keep a rental driver on the JAC flight
  • Tier 3 — refuse it: overnighting at DFW means Friday's first flight lands 10:19 — two hours after the van leaves. Before ~6 PM with no confirmed JAC seat → drive before you sleep
