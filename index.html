🌋 Saturday · Sep 19
Yellowstone + LSU/Ole Miss ▶
Don't try to "do" Yellowstone. Pick the 2–3 stops below and enjoy them rather than racing a loop. No cell service in the park — maps downloaded before you go.
❄️ Check Craig Pass before you roll (nps.gov/yell). The West Thumb → Old Faithful segment tops 8,262 ft and is the first park road to shut for snow — and we cross it twice, so a closure strands us on the far side. Unlikely (September averages 1.3" of snow) but free to check. If it's closed, swap to a Grand Teton morning — an hour closer, no new plan, back well before kickoff.
🕐 Two numbers run this day: leave at 6:15, and roll south out of West Thumb at 2:00 PM. It's ~1 hr 30 to the gate, ~1 hr 15 more to Old Faithful, and ~1 hr 50 back from West Thumb. Hold the 2:00 and you're home 3:50, grill lit 4:15, eating before kickoff.
