Misconnect Playbook ▶
Everyone's on one ticket, so AA rebooks free and automatically — the race is 13 seats to a small mountain airport, and the thing to protect is Friday's 8 AM van tour (refund window closed), not Thursday.
  • Tier 0 — prevention: all 13 load the trip in the AA app with push alerts; carry-on only if you can; sit toward the front of AA 468 — 43 min is a Skylink sprint

  • Tier 1 — missed it: catch the same-day ~5:50 PM DFW→JAC nonstop. All 13 rebook simultaneously in the app while one calls AA and one hits the gate desk. Hardie + Schick get seats first — they hold the rental cars
  • Tier 2 — no JAC seats: fly DFW→SLC and drive (~5 hrs); one-way SUV rentals SLC→Jackson are routine. Even a 3 PM SLC landing makes dinner at the house
  • Tier 3 — refuse this one: overnighting at DFW. Rule of thumb: before ~6 PM with no confirmed JAC seat, take the SLC routing. Drive before you sleep.
