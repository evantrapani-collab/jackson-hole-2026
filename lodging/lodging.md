### Location Notes

Teton Village is at the base of the ski mountain, about **20 minutes from downtown Jackson**. This works perfectly:

- **Mangy Moose** is right in Teton Village — basically walking distance from the house
- **Thursday night:** drive into Jackson for Snake River Brewing + Cowboy Bar, then end at Mangy Moose on the way back
- **Friday rafting:** Teton Village puts you closer to the Snake River put-in than downtown Jackson would
- **Grand Teton NP** entrances are just north — great staging point
- **Yellowstone** is ~1 hr north via Hwy 89

### Sleeping 13

**Confirmed bed layout** (from the RMR listing — [3D floor plan](https://www.rmrentals.com/jackson-hole-rentals/montreux-house)): 5 bedrooms / 6 beds = **5 kings + 1 queen**, spread across 3 levels.

| Level | Room | Bed(s) |
|---|---|---|
| Upper | Master Bedroom | 1 king |
| Main | Guest Bedroom | 1 queen |
| Lower | Guest Bedroom 1 | 1 king |
| Lower | Guest Bedroom 2 | 1 king |
| Lower | Junior Suite | 2 kings |

**The math for 13:** 6 beds. Even if *everyone* pairs up — 2 to each king, 2 to the queen — that maxes at 12. So **at least one person needs an air mattress or couch no matter what**, and realistically not everyone wants to share a king. Plan on **2 air mattresses** so you're not forcing six bed-shares.

A workable split (swap names to taste):

- **Upper — Master (king):** 1–2
- **Main — Guest (queen):** 1–2
- **Lower — Guest BR1 (king):** 2 shared
- **Lower — Guest BR2 (king):** 2 shared
- **Lower — Junior Suite (2 kings):** 4 (2 per king) + room for an air mattress — it's the biggest room and the obvious overflow spot

Note: 4 of the 6 beds (8 people) are on the **Lower Level** — the Junior Suite + both lower guest rooms cluster down there, with the Master up top and one queen on the main level.
