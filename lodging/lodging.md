# Lodging

## Jackson Hole (base for Grand Teton days)

### Top Picks

| Property | Vibe | Price Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Amangani** | Ultra-luxury, clifftop views | $$$$ | One of the best hotels in the US; book a year out |
| **Four Seasons Jackson Hole** | Ski resort luxury, mountain setting | $$$$ | At Teton Village, 20 min from town |
| **Rusty Parrot Lodge** | Boutique, downtown Jackson | $$$ | Excellent breakfast, walkable location |
| **Wort Hotel** | Classic western, historic | $$$ | On the town square; iconic Silver Dollar Bar |
| **Wyoming Inn** | Clean, comfortable, good value | $$ | Short drive to downtown |
| **Hostel X** | Budget bunk/private rooms | $ | At Teton Village; surprisingly nice |

### Guest Ranches (if you want the full experience)

- **Lost Creek Ranch** — all-inclusive, horses, exceptional
- **Heart Six Ranch** — more accessible price point, great for families
- **Spring Creek Ranch** — stunning ridge location with valley views

## Yellowstone (in-park lodging)

Book through **Xanterra** (the official concessionaire) at yellowstonenationalparklodges.com. September availability is tight — book 6–12 months in advance.

| Property | Location | Vibe | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Old Faithful Inn** | Upper Geyser Basin | Historic, iconic log lodge | Book the main inn, not the wings; opens in May |
| **Lake Yellowstone Hotel** | Yellowstone Lake | Elegant, colonial-style | Great sunrises over the lake |
| **Canyon Lodge** | Grand Canyon of Yellowstone | Modern cabins | Good central location |
| **Mammoth Hot Springs Hotel** | North end of park | Functional | Only Yellowstone lodge open year-round |

### Camping in Yellowstone

- **Slough Creek** (Lamar Valley) — best wildlife campground in the park; first-come, first-served
- **Pebble Creek** — quiet, beautiful, near Lamar
- **Lewis Lake** — southern park, more solitude
- Book reservable sites at recreation.gov

---

## Outside Yellowstone (alternatives to in-park)

- **Gardiner, MT** (North Entrance) — small town, good budget options
- **West Yellowstone, MT** (West Entrance) — more services, chain hotels
- **Cody, WY** (East Entrance) — farther but great western town
