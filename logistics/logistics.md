# Logistics

## Flights

### Airport Options

| Airport | Code | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Jackson Hole Airport | JAC | Inside Grand Teton NP; ~10 min to town. Convenient but expensive fares. |
| Salt Lake City | SLC | ~5 hr drive; often $300–500 cheaper round-trip. Scenic drive through Idaho. |
| Idaho Falls | IDA | ~2 hr drive; sometimes cheaper than JAC. Smaller airport. |

**Recommendation:** Check JAC first. If fares are >$300 more than SLC, do the SLC drive — it's pretty and the car rental will be cheaper there too.

### Booking Tips
- Book flights **6–9 months out** for September; fares spike in summer
- Set a Google Flights price alert now
- Southwest and United have the best JAC coverage; Delta connects through SLC

## Getting Around

### Car Rental

- **4WD or AWD strongly recommended** — park roads can have mud/snow in September at elevation
- Book a mid-size SUV minimum; full-size if 3+ people with luggage
- Book through the airport you fly into; one-way rentals between airports are expensive
- Popular options: Enterprise, Hertz, Avis at JAC; all major agencies at SLC
- **Book early** — September inventory in Jackson sells out fast

### Driving Notes

- Cell service: essentially zero in Yellowstone; download offline maps
- Gas up in Jackson before entering Grand Teton; gas inside parks is expensive
- Speed limits in parks are 25–45 mph; **wildlife jams** are common — budget extra time
- **Do not stop in the road for wildlife** — pull completely off, use designated pullouts
- Park operating hours: both parks are 24/7, but some roads and facilities close seasonally

### Park Driving Times (rough)

| Route | Drive Time |
|---|---|
| Jackson → Grand Teton South Entrance | 20 min |
| Jackson → Yellowstone South Entrance | 1 hr |
| Yellowstone South → Old Faithful | 30 min |
| Old Faithful → Lamar Valley | 1.5 hrs |
| Old Faithful → Mammoth Hot Springs | 45 min |
| Jackson → Grand Loop (full loop) | ~4 hrs without stops |

## National Park Passes & Entry Fees

| Option | Cost | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| **America the Beautiful Annual Pass** | $80 | Anyone visiting 2+ federal sites in a year; covers both parks |
| Grand Teton 7-day vehicle pass | $35 | Single park, single trip |
| Yellowstone 7-day vehicle pass | $35 | Single park, single trip |

**Get the America the Beautiful Pass.** It pays for itself instantly and covers all federal lands for a year.

Purchase at the park entrance, online at store.usgs.gov, or at REI.

## Permits & Reservations

### Yellowstone

- **Timed-entry permits** — NPS has experimented with these; check NPS.gov in early 2026. As of 2025, not required, but this may change.
- **Backcountry permits** — required for overnight trips; available at visitor centers
- **Fishing permit** — required (separate from the park pass); available at visitor centers

### Grand Teton

- No timed-entry permits as of 2025; monitor NPS.gov
- **Backcountry camping** — permit required; reservations open in January at recreation.gov

### Lodging Inside Parks

- **Yellowstone lodges** (Xanterra): book at yellowstonenationalparklodges.com; opens ~May for the following year
- **Grand Teton lodges** (Vail Resorts): gtlc.com; similar timeline

## Pre-Trip Checklist

### 9–12 Months Before (Now–Late 2025)

- [ ] Decide on exact dates
- [ ] Book Yellowstone lodging (books out fast)
- [ ] Book flights
- [ ] Reserve car rental

### 6 Months Before (Spring 2026)

- [ ] Get America the Beautiful Pass
- [ ] Check NPS.gov for timed-entry permit announcements
- [ ] Book Jackson lodging
- [ ] Book any guided activities (float trips, fishing, etc.)

### 1–2 Months Before

- [ ] Book Jackson restaurants
- [ ] Download offline maps (Gaia GPS, AllTrails, NPS apps)
- [ ] Confirm all reservations

### 1–2 Weeks Before

- [ ] Check weather forecast — pack accordingly
- [ ] Check park road/trail conditions at NPS.gov
- [ ] Print all confirmations (cell service will be absent)
- [ ] Notify credit card companies of travel

### On Arrival

- [ ] Buy/rent bear spray in Jackson or Gardiner
- [ ] Get Wyoming fishing license (if fishing) at local sporting goods store
- [ ] Grab groceries at Smith's or Albertsons before heading to parks
