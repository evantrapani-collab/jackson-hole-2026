# Packing List

**Warm afternoons (60s–70s), cold mornings (30s–40s), maybe rain.** Layers, not a parka. **Soft duffel, not a hard roller** — two SUVs, 13 guys and bags.

## Clothes

- [ ] **Puffy jacket** — both park mornings start in the 30s, in the dark, standing still. The one you'll be glad you brought
- [ ] **Rain shell**
- [ ] Fleece or hoodie
- [ ] T-shirts + a long-sleeve
- [ ] Hiking pants + shorts
- [ ] Wool or synthetic socks — **no cotton**
- [ ] **Boots, broken in** — Saturday is a 1.6-mile walk to the Prismatic overlook
- [ ] Sneakers
- [ ] One decent outfit for dinners in town
- [ ] Beanie, ball cap, sunglasses, gloves

## Friday — the raft day

- [ ] **Shoes that attach to your feet** — no flip-flops, they won't let you on
- [ ] **A dry set of clothes** for after the river
- [ ] Layers for the 8 AM safari — the van stops and everyone gets out

## Gear

- [ ] **Headlamp** — Sunday means setting up at Schwabacher in the dark, on a dirt path
- [ ] Daypack + water bottle
- [ ] Battery pack + car charger
- [ ] Sunscreen and SPF lip balm — altitude sun is intense
- [ ] Binoculars, if you've got them — Friday morning and Sunday dawn
- [ ] Your meds

## Documents & money

- [ ] Driver's license
- [ ] **Cash** — two guide tips Friday, plus the cash-only spots in town
- [ ] Offline maps + the Shaka Guide Yellowstone tour, **downloaded before you fly**

## Don't bring

- 🐻 **Bear spray** — you can't fly with it. We rent 2 canisters at the JAC kiosk on arrival
- An umbrella. Useless in mountain wind
